Topic: AI Agents

Summary: An AI agent is an artificial intelligence program that can make decisions, perceive its environment, and take actions to achieve specific goals. AI agents range from simple rule-based systems to adaptive learning models. They operate autonomously with minimal human intervention and are used in assistants, automation, and robotics.

Key points:
- Definition: Software systems that use AI to pursue goals and complete tasks on behalf of users (Google Cloud).
- Core loop: Perceive the environment, make decisions, and act to achieve goals.
- Types: Simple reflex agents, goal-based agents, and learning agents.
- Capabilities: Autonomous operation, access to data, interaction with applications and APIs, and reasoning.
- Applications: Virtual assistants, business process automation, robotics, and no-code agent building for busywork reduction.
